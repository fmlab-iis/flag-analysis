Protocol and FTEC CPU time estimates
==============================================================================

Protocol numbers are in combined_results_table.tex (Total CPU Time under SAT Solver).
FTEC numbers are in combined_results_table.tex (Total CPU Time under FTEC).

Protocol formula
----------------
  protocol_CPU
    = CF_export + CF_SAT
    + flag_Total_runtime
    + syndrome_Total_runtime

  CF_export ≈ JobRuntime(run_control_flow_dimacs.log)
              − max(JobRuntime in cnf_out_control_flow/<stem>/solve.log)

  CF_SAT           = Total SAT Time from control_flow_metrics_table.tex
  flag_Total_runtime = Time (s) from flag_raised_metrics_table.tex
  syndrome_Total_runtime = Total Syndrome Time from combined_sat_metrics_table.tex

Do not add flag/syndrome SAT on top of those Total runtimes (would double-count).

FTEC CPU time estimates
==============================================================================

Formula
-------
  FTEC_CPU = unified_export + unified_SAT

  unified_SAT = Total SAT Time from ftec_unified_metrics_table.tex
                (combined table uses 0.052 for Low-depth [[7,1,3]])

  unified_export (d=3):
    JobRuntime(run_unified_dimacs.log)
    − max(JobRuntime in cnf_out_unified/<stem>/solve.log)

  unified_export (d=5):
    run_unified_dimacs.log has no d=5 rows.
    Use CNF artifact mtime span in cnf_out_unified/<stem>/:
      max(mtime) − min(mtime) over path_*.cnf + path_*_var_map.json
    Lower bound (misses startup before first CNF).
